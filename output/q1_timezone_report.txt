
Timezone Operations Report

1. Original timezone:
The original patient_vitals dataset contained naive datetime objects.

2. Localization method:
Naive datetime indices were localized to UTC using tz_localize('UTC').

3. Conversion:
Converted to US/Eastern using tz_convert('US/Eastern').

4. DST handling:
UTC avoids ambiguity from daylight saving time transitions.
Example: March 12, 2023, 10:00:00 UTC converts to 06:00:00 in US/Eastern.

5. Recommendation:
Store temporal data in UTC internally, convert to local timezones only for reporting or visualization.
